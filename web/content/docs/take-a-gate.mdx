---
title: Take a gate
description: Read the handbook, answer three drawn questions, and get your fee back on a pass.
---

<Steps>
  <Step>
    **Read the handbook** on the gate page. Having it open while you answer is allowed and intended: the gate checks that you read and understood it, not that you memorised it.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Open an attempt.** Press **Take this gate**, connect a wallet, and enter your Discord id so the bot knows who passed. The page shows which three questions you will be dealt before you sign, and checks them against the contract's own deal.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Answer** each of the three questions in your own words, in any language, before the timer ends. Each answer is at most 600 characters. A short answer can pass. The rubric for each question is shown, so nobody has to guess what counts.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Submit**, then **Grade my answers**. Validators grade each answer, which takes about a minute, and the page shows **Validators are grading** with the transaction link while it waits.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **See the verdict.** PASSED, RETRY, LOCKED or EXPIRED, with the three labels, on the attempt page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens to your fee

| Result | Your fee |
|---|---|
| PASSED | credited back to you; take it out with **Withdraw** on the Passes page |
| RETRY | goes to the community's pool |
| LOCKED | goes to the community's pool |
| EXPIRED | goes to the community's pool |

A gate with a fee of zero has no money at all.

## If you do not pass

You may try again after the gate's cooldown (1 to 48 hours), up to three attempts in a round, and each try is dealt different questions. After the third failure the gate is closed to your address for seven days; then a new round of three starts.

## If the timer runs out

An attempt with no answer in time is **EXPIRED**. It counts as an attempt and its fee goes to the pool. Anyone can close it; the attempt page offers **Close attempt** once the time is up.
