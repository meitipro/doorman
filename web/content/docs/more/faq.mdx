## FAQ

**Is it a captcha?** No. A captcha checks for a pulse. Doorman checks that someone read the handbook.

**Can I read the handbook while I answer?** Yes. It is meant to be open next to the form.

**What if I fail?** You may try again after the cooldown, up to three attempts. A failed attempt's fee goes to the pool. After the third failure you wait seven days.

**Which network?** GenLayer Studio Next, chain 61997. Test GEN is free from the site's faucet.

**Why can anyone call `judge` and `expire`?** So a pass does not wait for the applicant to return, and an applicant who walked away cannot hold their seat. The caller is recorded.

**Can an owner move the bar on me?** No. An open attempt grades against the handbook and questions it was dealt.

**Can a pass be taken away?** No. Nothing in the contract revokes one.
