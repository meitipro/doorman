title: Introduction
description: Doorman is a reading gate for online communities, graded by GenLayer validators.

<Callout>A pass shows someone read the handbook. It does not show they are one person, and it cannot see what they do next.</Callout>

Every community has the same first day: a wave of new members, the same five questions asked in every channel, and a rule nobody read being broken within the hour. A captcha checks for a pulse. A fixed quiz gets posted in a group by the first person to pass. Doorman checks that a person **read the handbook**.

## What it does

1. A community writes its **handbook** (at most 3,000 characters) and a **bank of 6 to 20 questions**, each with a title (at most 100 characters) and a **rubric** (at most 300) saying what a passing answer must show.
2. An applicant opens an **attempt** with the gate's fee. Three questions are **drawn from their own address**, so two neighbours rarely get the same three.
3. They answer in their own words, in any language, inside the time limit (10 to 60 minutes).
4. GenLayer **validators** grade each answer against the handbook and its rubric: one label, `PASS` or `FAIL`, per answer.
5. The verdict is **computed in code** from the three labels and the pass mark the community wrote. A pass is recorded on chain, the fee comes back, and a read-only bot grants the role.

## What is on chain

Everything. The handbook, the bank, every attempt's questions, answers, labels and verdict are public text on [Studio Next](/docs/reference/addresses). That is deliberate: a gate that waves everyone through is visible in its pass rate, and the rubrics can be read without being gamed.
